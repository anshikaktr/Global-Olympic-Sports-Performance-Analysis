About this project

The Olympic Games show how nations invest in sport, but the data runs to over 166,000 athlete entries. These dashboards help national committees, analysts and journalists see where medals are won, how participation has changed and which regions convert athletes into medals.

Medal leaders

The United States leads with 1,222 medals, followed by China (759) and Germany (726).

Summer and Winter

Summer Games award 815 to 1,080 medals per edition; Winter Games 171 to 349.

Regional efficiency

From 1992 to 2016, Europe/Asia won about 14 medals per 100 athletes sent, South America about 3.
